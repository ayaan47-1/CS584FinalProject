No persistent model checkpoints are included with this submission. All experiments
are reproducible by running the cells of src/patchtst.ipynb in order. Training
results are cached to results.json after each run, allowing the notebook to skip
re-training completed experiments on subsequent kernel sessions.

Each model takes approximately 8 seconds per epoch on Apple M4 Pro (MPS backend),
with early stopping (patience=10) typically terminating between 20–40 epochs. A
full reproduction of all main results and ablations completes in roughly 30 minutes.

To re-train from scratch, delete results.json before running the notebook.
