Datasets used in this project:

ETT (Electricity Transformer Temperature) datasets — ETTh1 and ETTh2
Source: https://github.com/zhouhaoyi/ETDataset
Direct download links:
  ETTh1: https://github.com/zhouhaoyi/ETDataset/raw/main/ETT-small/ETTh1.csv
  ETTh2: https://github.com/zhouhaoyi/ETDataset/raw/main/ETT-small/ETTh2.csv

Files are included in the data/ folder of this submission (each ~2.5 MB, well under the 10MB limit).

Citation:
H. Zhou, S. Zhang, J. Peng, S. Zhang, J. Li, H. Xiong, and W. Zhang,
"Informer: Beyond Efficient Transformer for Long Sequence Time-Series Forecasting,"
AAAI, 2021.
